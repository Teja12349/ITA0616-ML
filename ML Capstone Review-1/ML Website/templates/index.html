Academic Capstone Specifications & Problem Overview

Problem Statement

Industrial pumps and compressors frequently suffer catastrophic failures due to abnormal pressure drops, flow turbulence, thermal breakdown, and vibration friction. Traditional scheduled maintenance often detects flaws too late after severe hardware degradation. This capstone project integrates fluid dynamics physics formulas with machine learning pipelines to detect early anomaly signatures and prevent unplanned downtime.

Key Project Objectives
  1. Acquire and clean real-time fluid dynamic telemetry parameters.
  2. Perform missing value imputation and noise filtering.
  3. Engineer fluid features (Head, Pressure Drop, Vibration RMS).
  4. Train Decision Tree, Random Forest, and XGBoost models.
  5. Provide interactive executive dashboard with risk alerts.
Expected Outcome

A fully functional, web-based predictive maintenance application capable of continuously ingesting sensor data, computing fluid dynamic indicators, predicting failure risk percentages, classifying equipment into NORMAL, WARNING, or POTENTIAL FAILURE states, and dispensing automated repair prescriptions.
